# Using `route=hiking` relations to discover pre-canned trips (Nov 18, 2026)


One thing I want from a hiking app is for it to suggest "pre-canned" trips.

It's not essential. I'm comfortable planning trips on my own from a topographic map. Still, it's nice to get a quick overview of what's available in an area.

So... is that easy? Maybe they already exist in OSM as [route=hiking](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:route%3Dhiking) relations?

I did a quick sanity check, using [Killarney Provincial Park](/hikes/canada/ontario/killarney-provincial-park), as I'm somewhat familiar with it.

It has two very famous hikes, the multi-day [La Cloche Silhouette trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Cloche_Silhouette_Trail) and the day-trip to [the Crack](https://blog.ontarioparks.ca/hike-the-crack-killarney/). The park's website also lists [four other hikes along named trails](https://www.ontarioparks.ca/park/killarney/activities).

The results weren't encouraging:

- ✅ La Cloche had a `route=hiking` relation.
- ❌ The Crack had a `route=hiking` relation, but it was wrong -- it only included the first of several ways.
- ❌ None of Chikanishing Trail, Cranberry Bog Trail, Granite Ridge Trail or Lake of the Woods Trail had relations.

I submitted a fix for the Crack, but this suggested that relying on `route=hiking` would be disappointing.

I spotchecked ~100 other hikes in Canadian parks and the pattern held up: almost all had named trails in OSM, but only ~12% had a relation that followed them.

So maybe you can use the presence of a `route=hiking` relation to suggest a route, but you can't rely on it.

So... what to do? That's a topic for another time.
