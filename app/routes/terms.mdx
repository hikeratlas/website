# Terms and privacy

_Last updated November 14, 2026. (Return to the [homepage](/).)_

## Who runs Hiker Atlas

Hiker Atlas is a free hobby project by Colin Dellow. You can reach me at
[cldellow@gmail.com](mailto:cldellow@gmail.com). Its code is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/hikeratlas).

## No warranty

Hiker Atlas is offered as is, with no warranty of any kind. I'm not liable for
anything that happens from using it, as far as the law allows.

## Hiking is risky: you're responsible for your own safety

- **Hiking has inherent risks.** You're responsible for your own safety when
  you're outdoors.
- **Hiker Atlas isn't a substitute for experience.** It doesn't replace the
  experience, skills, gear and judgement to keep yourself safe. Check
  conditions, closures and the weather before you go, and turn back when you
  should.
- **Maps can be wrong.** They're drawn from public sources, chiefly
  [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org), that may be inaccurate or out
  of date. Trails and conditions on the ground may not match the map. The
  suggested hikes are picked by a computer, not walked by me, and times,
  distances and difficulty are estimates.

## Things you post are public

Hiker Atlas's community features, like faving a hike and (in future) reviews
and GPS tracks, work through your
[AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) account: the same kind of account as
[Bluesky](https://bsky.app).

- **Your posts live in your account, not on Hiker Atlas's servers.** Each fave,
  review or track is saved as a public record in your own account, on your
  account's server (your PDS), and anyone can see it, here or in any other app.
  Your profile page here (`/people/your.handle`) just reads it from there.
- **You can delete them,** in Hiker Atlas (tap the heart again to unfave) or
  directly through your account's server. As on any public network, copies
  other services made while a record was public may outlive its deletion.
- **Signing in** happens at your account's server (Bluesky's, for most people).
  Hiker Atlas never sees your password. It asks only to write its own kinds of
  record (`com.hikeratlas.*`) and to upload files (GPS tracks and photos),
  not to read your messages or post as you elsewhere.

<h2 id="privacy">Privacy</h2>

- **No accounts, no analytics.** Hiker Atlas has no accounts of its own and
  doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies.
- **Your sign-in stays in your browser.** Signing in keeps a session in your
  browser (IndexedDB), and only there. Signing out removes it.
- **A few settings are kept in your browser** (local storage): your recent
  searches, and whether you turned on aerial imagery. They never leave your
  device.
- **The services the site uses see the usual details of each request,** like
  your IP address and browser:
  - Hiker Atlas's hosting (Cloudflare) serves the site, its maps and its routes.
  - Search goes to Hiker Atlas's search service (on Amazon Web Services). It
    sends what you typed and roughly where the map is (to about 100 m), to
    rank nearby results first.
  - Terrain comes from [Mapterhorn](https://mapterhorn.com). Aerial imagery,
    if you turn it on, comes from EOX, the USGS, and the governments of
    Ontario, Quebec and Saskatchewan. The map loads a script from unpkg,
    and fonts come from Google Fonts.
  - "Directions to the trailhead" opens Google Maps.

  Hiker Atlas doesn't combine or keep these details beyond its providers'
  ordinary logs.
